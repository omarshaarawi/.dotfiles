# Logo Clouds

Covers: logo grids, customer logos, partner logos, trust bars, client rows, and collections of brand marks.

- Always distribute logos evenly across rows when wrapping — never allow an unbalanced last
  row (5 on one row and 1 on the next); split them as evenly as possible (3+3 for 6 logos)
- Logo clouds directly beneath a hero are an extension of the hero — match the hero's
  alignment; a left-aligned hero gets a left-aligned logo cloud label and logos
